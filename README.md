# ISLND

A third-person voxel sandbox with a smooth, toy-like look (soft rounded block textures, round characters), in one HTML file, plus a small Node server for multiplayer rooms.

**The ISLND look.** The start screen is a black-and-white poster: ISLND right across the top, *WASD — Control your character.*, and a black ribbon button to play. Everything in the game is black and white with sharp edges: the HUD, toasts, chat, the hotbar and block picker, the shop and Pemberton's menu, the map, name tags and speech bubbles. Big headers use **HORIZON** and everything else, speech included, uses **Cy Grotesk V3 Key**. Neither is a free web font, so the game uses them when they're installed on the computer, or when the font files are put in `public/fonts/` (`horizon.woff2` or `.otf`, and `cy-grotesk-key.woff2` or `.otf`). Otherwise it uses the nearest free fonts: Unbounded for HORIZON and Lexend Giga for Cy Grotesk Key. Saves still use the old `voxel-isle-` names, so nobody loses their island.

The camera sits over your right shoulder and pulls in when a block is in the way. Press **V** (or the **View** touch button) to switch to first person. Aiming casts from the camera through the crosshair, limited to 6 blocks from the player.

**Customise your character** on the start card: colours for skin, hair, shirt, trousers, shoes and hat, plus hair, face and hat styles, with a 3D preview you can drag to turn. Your look saves in your browser. In a room everyone sees it, and changes show up live (the server keeps only known colours and style names).

**Rod creatures.** Set Type to **Rod creature** in the customiser for a floating cube head you paint yourself (16×16 pixels with a 16-colour palette: pen, fill, pick, mirror, undo, clear; change a palette colour and every pixel using it updates). Show the painting on the front only or on all sides. Pick a rod style (spinning **pillars**, a whirling **swirl**, swaying **tendrils**, or capped **legs** that step as you walk), a rod colour, and an effect (embers, wind, bubbles, sparkles or smoke). Ember, Gale, Tide and Slate are starting points. Painted heads sync in rooms; the server only accepts a 16-colour palette and 256 hex digits.

**Underground.** Digging down goes through varied topsoil (with clay pockets) into rock layers that wave and tilt across the island: stone with bands of granite and clay, gravel pockets near the top, sandstone under beaches, blue deepstone with basalt near the bottom, and ragged bedrock you can't break. Coal, copper and iron ore are scattered through the rock, with rare glowing crystal in the deepstone. Closed caves sit under the meadow and the mountain. Light fades as you go under: sky light spills a few blocks into holes and caves, lamps and crystals glow, and characters darken too. Gravel falls like sand. Middle-click any block you find to put it on your hotbar. The meadow's surface layer can't be broken by anyone (the server enforces it too), but everything under it can: dig down beside the meadow and tunnel underneath.

**The island jet.** Bird Air's jumbo: a fat double-decker whose white skin runs smoothly from a long, pointed, slightly drooping nose (with a curved row of cockpit windows) to a long upswept tail. Two rows of windows and doors run down each side, **BIRD AIR** is painted in big blue letters along the top deck, the belly is pale grey, and a deep blue sweep with a sky-blue edge curves up round the back of the body to the fin. It has long, low, swept wings with a kinked trailing edge, fences at the tips, flap fairings and a smooth wing-to-body fairing. Four big engines hang on pylons under the wings, each with a chrome lip, a dark intake with a spinner, and an exhaust cone that glows. The tall swept fin is deep blue, with sky-blue sweeps, a white bird and **JUMBO** on it. There are tailplanes low on the tail cone, blinking beacons, red and green wingtip lights, landing lights, and big bogies of wheels with gear doors that fold away once it's flying clear of the ground. It flies a tour between four plank docks: **East Beach** on the old island (its home), **North Shore** on the eastern land near the treehouse, **South Shore** below the cottage, and **West Bay** on the southern land. Each dock has a sign. On autopilot it waits at home, then flies high over the sea and hills to each dock in turn, lands, waits 14 seconds and moves on, then flies home: about four and a half minutes a tour, timed from a shared clock so everyone in a room sees it in the same place. Walk up to it at any dock and press **E** (or **Board** on touch); the hint says where it goes next and when it leaves. **Calling the jet:** stand on any dock and press **E** (or **Call** on touch), or ring the gold bell on the post there. The jet leaves its tour and flies straight to you, high over the land, lands and waits 25 seconds; then it flies back to East Beach and picks up its tour. The hint and the cabin screen say it's coming. Anyone in a room can call it (the server keeps everyone's flight the same), but not while someone is flying it.

**On board the jumbo** you walk about (always in first person): **W**/**A**/**S**/**D** to walk, **Shift** to hurry, and the view turns with the jet. You come aboard standing by the front door on the left.
- **Economy** fills the main deck: ten rows of blue seats, two each side of a carpeted aisle, windows down both sides and ceiling lights. Walk up to a seat and press **E** to sit down, and **E** again to stand up.
- **Business class** is the whole top deck, up the stairs at the back of the cabin. It's for **Hoot's and Snowy's owners** (and Slinky's owner, who goes everywhere owl owners do); everyone else gets told economy is lovely too. Up there are six lie-flat business pods in cream shells, each with its own screen and lamp (**E** to sit in one), and a wooden bar with a gold top at the front: **E** at the bar for bubbly (sparkling apple juice), strawberries and cream, a pot of tea or a juice box.
- **Getting off:** walk to the front door and press **E**. In the air you jump out and your parachute opens.
- **Up front** is the cockpit: the pilot's seat, a dashboard of live gauges (speed, altitude, a compass and the throttle, plus where it's heading), a yoke that turns as the jet banks, a throttle lever, and the whole nose to see out of. A screen on the front wall of the cabin shows the next stop.
- In a room other players see you in one of the four seats by the door, wherever you've walked to. The host and admins (and you, in single player) take the pilot's seat: **W**/**S** throttle, the mouse steers, **Space**/**Shift** go up and down, **E** leaves. Everyone else rides as a passenger (up to four). When the pilot leaves, the jet flies back to East Beach, lands and waits for its next departure. The server only lets the host or an admin fly, only takes flight updates from the current pilot, and takes the controls away if admin is removed.

**Pets.** Ten pets live around the island, and you can have one at a time: owls **Hoot** and **Snowy** perch on the roof of the big treehouse on the eastern land, **Goldie** the fish swims round a little glass house on the sea bed out west of West Bay (x 50, z 226), **Biscuit** the puppy and **Mochi** the kitten wait on cushions under the arbour at the gate of their cottage at the far south of the southern land, **Cinder** the dragon sleeps in its den under the south beach (a stairway leads down from the meadow's south edge), and **Clover** the horse grazes in a paddock on the neck of land between the eastern and southern lands, by the road. Walk up and press **E** to adopt one; adopting another sends the first home. Pets follow you everywhere: walkers trot along and paddle across water, fliers keep to your shoulder, the fish floats along in a bubble, and they all catch up if you get far ahead or come along in the jet. The pause card lists each pet with a hint, and **Send home**. Your pet is saved with your look; in a room everyone sees it, and the server keeps each pet to one player.

**Right click without a right button.** Ctrl and click does everything a right click does (placing blocks, opening doors, sleeping, writing on signs), for Mac trackpads and one-button mice.

**Beds, doors and the block picker.** Press **B** (or the **+** at the end of the hotbar) to open the block picker: a panel of rounded 3D blocks with every block in the game, including the new **Bed**, **Door** and twelve plain colours (red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, pink, brown, black, grey and white). Pick one and it goes in your selected hotbar slot; the hotbar shows 3D blocks too. A bed is two blocks long (the head goes the way you're facing) and half a block high, with a wooden frame, a red quilt, a pillow and a headboard; you can stand on it. A door is two blocks tall and sits between the walls either side of it. Right click (or **E**) on a door to open or shut it; open, it swings back against the wall and you can walk through. Breaking either half of a bed or door takes the whole thing. Anyone can open and shut a door, even in a house that can't be changed, and the server allows exactly that and nothing more.

**Sleeping.** Right click (or **E**) on a bed to lie down. Night falls over the screen with stars and a moon, then morning comes, and that bed becomes where you wake up: falling off the world or pressing **R** puts you back beside it (if it's gone, you wake on the meadow). Move, jump or press **E** to get up. In a room others see you asleep in the bed. There's a bed for the owner in every pet's home: in each owl's bedroom in the treehouse, in Goldie's dome, in Cinder's den, in Clover's stable, and in Biscuit's and Mochi's bedrooms upstairs in the cottage, which now has a front door and a door into each bedroom.

**The campsite.** Just south of Clover's paddock, by the road, a campsite has three tents (orange, blue and green) with a sleeping bag in each, round a campfire with a stone ring, a glowing pit and flickering flames, plus log benches, lanterns on posts at the corners, a woodpile and a sign. Anyone can sleep there. The campsite itself can't be changed.

**Fishing.** Stand on any jet dock and press **Q** (or **Fish** on touch) to cast; a red and white bobber lands on the water in front of you. When it dips, press **Q** quickly to reel in, or it gets away. There are twelve fish in four rarities (common, uncommon, rare and legendary), and each dock has a rare fish of its own: the glow eel at East Beach, the icefin at North Shore, the angelfish at South Shore and the clownfish at West Bay. A caught fish leaps out of the water to you. Everything you catch goes in the **fish book** on the pause card (kept in your browser), with ??? and a hint for the ones you haven't found. In a room others see your line and bobber.

**The island map.** Press **M** (or **Map**, or the button on the pause card) for a map of the whole island drawn from the blocks: the homes, the meadow, the campsite, all four docks, your bed, the jet, other players with their names, and you as an arrow pointing the way you face.

**Playing together.**
- **Chat:** press **Enter** to type and Enter to send. Your words pop up in a bubble over your head and in the chat log; the server keeps them short and plain.
- **Emotes:** press **X**, then **1** wave, **2** dance, **3** sit, **4** cheer or **5** clap (or click one), or type /wave, /dance and so on in chat. Moving stops them; everyone sees them. Rod creatures emote their own way: they wave a tendril (or tilt and nod their head), spin round to dance, squash down to sit, bounce with a spinning head to cheer (their effect bursting out), and pulse their head to clap.
- **Pet tricks:** press **Y** and your pet sits, spins round, jumps for joy or dances, a different one each time, and others see it too.
- **Tag:** the host starts a two-minute game from **Games** on the pause card. Whoever is it has a red IT! sign over their head; touch someone to pass it on (no tagging straight back). When time's up, the least time spent as it wins. The server checks every tag.
- **The horse race:** **Race to the campsite** puts you on the start line at the meadow, counts down 3, 2, 1, GO, and times you through six glowing rings over the bridge and down the road to the campsite (ride Clover for the best time). Your best time is kept. In a room the host starts a race for everyone, and finishing places are announced; the server won't accept impossible times.
- **Holding the jet:** when the jet is waiting at a dock, the host or an admin (or you, alone) can **Hold the jet** there from **Games**, so everyone has time to board, then **Send the jet on**. Nobody else can call it away while it's held.

**Goldie's and Cinder's powers.**
- **Goldie, T: Bubble Dash.** For 30 seconds you swim inside a bubble with Goldie leading the way: three times as fast, in any direction (**Space** and **Shift** to rise and dive), and **Space** near the surface leaps you right out of the water.
- **Goldie, G: Lucky Fin.** Your next five casts get a bite almost at once, and rare and legendary fish are much more likely. (Goldie needs a rest before the next one.)
- **Cinder, T: Dragon Flight.** You climb on Cinder's back and fly for 30 seconds, faster than anything else on the island; **T** again to land. Everyone sees you riding the dragon.
- **Cinder, G: Fire Breath.** Cinder breathes a burst of flame the way you face. It melts ice in front of you, and if Biscuit or Mochi is chasing you, they run off from the flames. Others in a room see the fire.

**Hoot and Snowy's powers.** If Hoot or Snowy is your pet, press **T** (or **Lift me up** on the pause card) and your owl grabs you by the shoulders and flies you about for 30 seconds: look where you want to go, **W** to fly, **Space** and **Shift** to climb and dive, faster than flying on your own. The hint counts down. Press **T** again, or run out of time, and the owl glides you gently down. Each owl also has a power of its own on **G** (or its button on the pause card), lasting 20 seconds, with a short rest before the next go:

**Slinky the snake.** A green snake with a yellow diamond back lives coiled among the roots under the owls' treehouse, by a hollow log. Its owner counts as an owl owner everywhere: they can go into the treehouse and the Sky Palace, the members' lounge, Pemberton serves them, Bazza never eggs them, the helicopter is theirs to fly, and the secret lab's door opens for them. Slinky has all the owls' powers:
- **T** lifts you up and flies you about, with Slinky coiled round your shoulders.
- **G** is Snake Senses: Owl Eyes and Frost Wings at once (see in the dark and through rock, and freeze the sea under your feet).

The one thing Slinky's owner can't do is use the gadgets in the secret lab: they can go in and look, but the gadgets are for Hoot's and Snowy's owners. /tp slinky (or /tp snake) goes to the treehouse.
- **Hoot's Owl Eyes:** you see in the dark (caves and tunnels light up), and ores and crystals within 16 blocks glow through the rock in their colours, along with other players and pets. An amber glow round the screen shows it's on.
- **Snowy's Frost Wings:** the water freezes into ice under your feet as you go, so you can walk or run straight across the sea and lakes (if you're swimming, you come up onto the ice). The ice melts back after 15 seconds and is never saved. In a room everyone sees the ice form under you. Ice is also a new block in the picker.

Both work for builders in a room, and everyone sees you hanging from your owl with your arms up.

**East Beach Airport.** Just south of the East Beach jet dock, on stilts over the shallow sea, is the island's airport. A plank walkway runs from the dock (and one from the beach) to the **terminal**: white walls, a glass front looking out at the runway, a flat roof with a blue rim and the airport's name over the door. Inside are check-in desks, rows of seats, plants and the **departures and arrivals board**, which shows the jet's tour as four flights (IS1 East Beach to North Shore, IS2 to South Shore, IS3 to West Bay, IS4 home), soonest first, with live countdowns and statuses: Boarding while the jet waits at a dock, Departed and In flight on the way, Landed when it arrives, Delayed when someone calls it away, and Private flight when someone is flying it. Behind the terminal stands a **control tower** with a glass cab and a light on top, and past the apron (with a yellow taxiway line) is a long asphalt **runway** with white edge lines, edge lights, a dashed centre line and stripes at each end.
- **Air traffic control.** The room's host and admins (or you, on your own) can route the jet from anywhere: **Air traffic control** on the pause card opens the island map with the tower's controls. Click the map to add up to 8 waypoints (**Undo** and **Clear route** fix mistakes), then press **Land at** a dock. The jet leaves whatever it was doing, flies through each waypoint high over the land (under the Sky Palace), lands at that dock and waits, then flies home to East Beach and picks up its tour. Everyone sees the route on their map, the tower tells everyone where the jet's been cleared to, and the airport board says the jet is on a special route. In a room the server only accepts a route from the host or an admin, and nobody can route the jet while someone is flying it.
- **The members' lounge** at the back of the terminal, behind a glass wall, is for the owners of **Hoot, Snowy, Biscuit, Mochi and Clover**. It has a purple carpet, sofas, a snack bar, a coffee table, plants, a hanging lamp and a big window over the sea, and members can redecorate it. Anyone else is stopped at the door (and put back in the hall if they end up inside). In a room the server checks who may decorate it.
- **The jet takes off from the runway.** When the tour leaves East Beach, the jet turns on its dock, taxis along the taxiway (follow the yellow line) onto the runway's north end, taxis down to the far end, turns round, rolls north faster and faster, lifts off near the end and climbs away out over the sea. The board shows flight IS1 as Taxiing, then Taking off, then Departed. (It still lands at the dock when it comes home, and when it's called it flies straight to and from the docks.)
- The rest of the airport can't be changed.

**Chat commands.** Press **Enter** and type a command instead of a message (**/help** lists them all in the chat log; commands aren't said out loud):
- **/spawn** *[name]* puts a **test dummy** in front of you: an orange-vested mannequin in a yellow cap with a name label, which drops to the ground and stands there. It's for trying things out, and the helicopter's rope can snatch it up too. **/despawn** removes the one nearest you and **/clear** removes them all (up to 12 at a time; they aren't saved).
- **/spawn jet** and **/spawn heli** (or *helicopter*) bring the island's jet or helicopter to you, parked on the ground just in front of you: the jet pointing the way you look, the helicopter with its nose towards you. The jet waits there until someone flies it, calls it to a dock, or **/despawn jet** sends it flying home to East Beach (the departures board shows it as a private flight meanwhile). The helicopter stays where it's put, like always, until someone flies it or **/despawn heli** puts it back on its pad at North Shore. Neither can be moved while someone is flying it. In a room only the host and admins can do this, and everyone sees it.
- **On phones and tablets** tap **Chat**: the box opens at the top of the screen (so the keyboard doesn't cover it) with taps for common commands underneath — /help, /spawn, /spawn jet, /spawn heli, /despawn, /fly, /home, /where. Tap Chat again, or close the keyboard, to put it away. Flying the helicopter adds **Rope**, **Let go** and **Bridge** buttons.
- **/tp** *x y z* teleports you to a spot, and **/tp** *name* to a player or a dummy. **/tp jet** and **/tp heli** take you to the jet and the helicopter (standing beside them; aboard the jet if it's flying, or by the helicopter's door with your parachute open if it's hanging in the air), and **/tp** and a pet's name (or its home's: *hoot*, *snowy* or *treehouse*, *goldie*, *biscuit*, *mochi* or *cottage*, *cinder* or *den*, *clover* or *paddock*) to that pet's home. Cinder's den takes you to its arch when Cinder belongs to someone else. **/home** takes you back to your bed or the meadow, and **/fly** starts or stops flying.
- **/pet** *name* adopts a pet (**/pet none** sends yours home), **/jet** *dock* calls the jet (east, north, south or west), **/block** *name* puts a block in your hand, and **/where** says where you are.
- The emotes work too: **/wave**, **/dance**, **/sit**, **/cheer**, **/clap**.
- In a room only the host and admins can spawn and clear dummies (the server checks), and everyone sees the same ones. The rest work for anyone. Nothing gets you out of the helicopter if you've been kidnapped.

**Host and admin tools.** On your own island you can use all of these; in a room only the host and admins can (the server checks), and everyone shares the result:
- **/time** *day, night, dawn, dusk, noon, midnight*, or a time like *20:30* or *7pm*. The sky light dims and warms with the hour: golden at dawn and dusk, dark blue at night with stars and a moon, while lamps and crystals keep glowing and caves look the same. **/time cycle** lets the day run on (a whole day every 20 minutes); **/time stop** holds it. Sleeping in a bed at night on your own island brings the morning.
- **/weather** *clear, rain, snow* or *storm*. Rain and snow fall round you (not under a roof or under water); a storm darkens the sky and flashes with lightning.
- **/announce** *message* shows a big message across everyone's screen, with who sent it.
- **/freeze** *name* (or *all*) freezes a player in a block of ice: they can't move, build, ride or use commands until **/unfreeze** *name*. The host can't be frozen, and only the host can freeze admins.
- **/give** *name block* (or *all*) puts a block in someone's hand.
- **/paste** and **/undo** (below) in a room.

**Building tools** (for everyone):
- **/lock** *[size]* locks the build round you, from just under your feet up to the sky, 21 blocks across by default (up to 33, or 65 for the host and admins). Only you and the host can change anything in it; gold posts mark it out. **/unlock** while standing in it lifts it (the host and admins can lift anyone's; **/unlock all**), and **/locks** lists them. Up to 3 each; they can't overlap someone else's; they go when you leave the room.
- **/pos1** and **/pos2** mark two opposite corners (the block you're looking at, or *x y z*), shown by a blue box. **/copy** copies everything between them (up to 64 each way), **/rotate** turns the copy a quarter turn, and **/paste** puts it down where you're looking (or at *x y z*); add *air* to paste the empty spaces too. It skips anything fixed or locked. **/undo** takes the last paste back.
- **/save** *name* keeps the copy in this browser, **/load** *name* brings it back (any day, on any island), **/builds** lists them and **/forget** *name* deletes one.

**Fireworks.** **/fireworks** sets off a show of a dozen rockets in front of you: they climb on a spark trail and burst into balls, rings and crackles of colour. **/fireworks palace** launches them over the Sky Palace. Anyone can, every few seconds, and in a room everyone sees the same show. Best at night.

**The cable car.** From the summit of the southern peaks (a station on a stone plinth, up three steps from the snow) down to a station just north of Biscuit and Mochi's cottage hedge. Two cabins, one red and one blue, run on two ropes: while one waits 10 s at the summit the other waits at the bottom, then they pass each other half way. Stand by a waiting cabin and press **E** to get in; you can't get out half way, and at the other end you step out on to the platform. Both stations are fixed. It runs in the spread layout (where the cottage is by the peaks); everyone sees the same cabins, since they run on the room's clock.

**Jet skis.** The pond below the campsite is dug through to the West Bay lake and on to the sea. Steps lead down from the camp to a jetty with lamp posts, where five jet skis (yellow No. 1, green No. 2, purple No. 3, pink No. 4 and red No. 5) are moored. Walk up to one and press **E**: **W** and **S** for the throttle (and reverse), **A** and **D** (or the stick) to steer, **E** to get off. Each is a sleek white hull with a pointed bow under a coloured deck and nose cowl, with a tinted windscreen, a long black seat, handlebars with mirrors, foot wells, a swim platform and its racing number on both sides. They throw up spray, can't drive up on to land, and stay where you leave them (even after a reload). In a room everyone sees everyone's, one rider each. **/despawn skis** (host and admins) sends the free ones back to the jetty.

**Skiing.** A ski rack stands on each cable car station: press **E** there to put on skis (or take them off), or type **/ski** anywhere. On skis you slide downhill with the slope and pick up speed: steer with the mouse or **A** and **D** (the edges grip, so you go where the skis point), **W** pushes with your poles, **S** snowploughs to a stop, and a step up at speed throws you into a little jump. Snow and ice are slippery; grass and the rest slow you down. **E** takes them off. Ride the cable car up, ski down to the cottage. Everyone sees your skis and poles.

**Slabs, stairs and slides.** All in the block picker (**B**). **Slabs** (wood or stone) are half a block high. **Stairs** (wood or stone) go down facing the way you look, so you walk straight up them away from you. You step up half a block without jumping, so stairs and slabs are just walked up. A **slide** is a smooth red ramp, high at your end and sloping down the way you face: step on and you whizz down it, faster and faster, steering a little as you go.

**Crashing the jet.** Fly the jet into the island fast (nose, tail or a wing first), slam it down hard on its belly, or dive it into the sea, and it blows up: a fireball, sparks, black smoke, bits of jet flying everywhere, a flash and a shake. Everyone aboard wakes up on the beach at the East Beach dock, and a few seconds later the jet is back at home. Slow, gentle landings are fine. In a room everyone sees the explosion. The helicopter never crashes: it just bumps.

**The Island Shop.** A wooden kiosk with a striped red and white awning, balloons and goods on the shelves stands just south of where you start, kept by Polly the parrot, who watches you and squawks. Your coins show at the top left: you start with 25, earn more by catching fish (2 to 30 each, by rarity), cracking Bazza's eggs (3), finishing a race (15) and calling in at the shop once a day (20). Press **E** by Polly to shop: an ice cream (all your hearts back, and a cone to hold), a pet treat (a happy trick), a party balloon (hold it for a minute and you float down gently from anywhere; others see it), a confetti popper, and four hats for you to keep: a top hat, a party hat, a pirate hat and a cowboy hat. Bought hats unlock in the skin menu (the others show a lock), and every hat fits rod creatures' box heads as well as people; rod creatures hold their ice cream and tea beside their heads. **Esc** or **E** closes the shop. Coins and hats are kept in your browser.

**The trampoline.** A big round trampoline with a padded blue rim, springs and a little ladder stands on the meadow just west of where you start. Walk on to it or drop on to it and you bounce, a bit higher each time; hold **Space** as you land for an extra boost (up to about 14 blocks high), or **Shift** to stop bouncing and stand on the mat. The mat dips as you land. Double-tapping Space doesn't start you flying while you're on it.

**Bazza, Biscuit and Mochi's ostrich.** A big ostrich with a long pink neck, huge eyelashes and a cork hat hangs about on the path outside the cottage gate, doing odd jobs for Biscuit's and Mochi's owners in a broad Aussie accent ("G'day boss!", "No wukkas, mate", "Strewth!"). When the porta-potty floods the front garden he grabs a mop and cleans it up, so it drains away in 20 seconds instead of two minutes; he gets on with it by himself after a few seconds, or straight away if one of his bosses presses **E** by him. Every few minutes he legs it off to lay a clutch of giant ostrich eggs at someone's place (by their bed if they have one, or wherever they are) and runs back: the eggs are big and in the way until you crack them (anyone can, anywhere). He never lays them for Hoot's and Snowy's owners (they'd have his guts) or his own bosses, and his bosses can send him off with **E**. Anyone else who bothers him is told to rack off. In a room the host's game sends him off, so everyone sees the same run and the eggs land for everyone.

**Things that go wrong.** Three people on one zip line at once and it snaps, dropping everyone off (a short end of cable dangles from each post until it's mended 30 seconds later). In a storm the lighthouse lamp blows its fuse: sparks, and the beam goes dark until the storm passes. Stand under a circling flock of seagulls for too long and one poops on you: a white splat on your head (and over your eyes in first person) that everyone in a room can see, until you jump in the water to wash it off. And if Biscuit or Mochi stays in food form near the seagulls, one swoops down and carries it off over the sea, decides it isn't food after all, and drops it; your pet runs home as itself again.

**Pigeon seed.** Press **N** (or type **/seeds**) to scatter seed on the ground in front of you: every pigeon about flies in, and while they eat they crowd round you and stay put even when you're right next to them (unless you run at them).

**Bird Air says sorry.** After a crash Bird Air posts everyone aboard a letter: it arrives in your Bird Air mailbox at home (by the bed you last slept in, or else at your pet's house: the bottom of the treehouse stairs, the cottage gate, Cinder's den, Clover's paddock or floating above Goldie's house, or else on the meadow by where you start), whose red flag goes up; walk up to it and press **E** to read it. **Redstone.** The block picker has a set of redstone parts for building circuits:

- **Spark wire** lies flat on the ground and joins up with the wire and parts beside it. It also runs up or down one block, so it can climb a staircase. It's a dull blue-green, and glows bright blue-green when it carries power, and the power gets one weaker each block, so a signal reaches 15 blocks.
- **Lever:** right-click to flip it on or off.
- **Button:** right-click to press it. It stays on for a second, then pops back up.
- **Pressure plate:** on while anyone stands on it.
- **Redstone block:** always on.
- **Redstone lamp:** lights up when powered.
- **NOT gate:** on unless its back is powered. It points the way you face when you place it, and powers the block in front.
- **Delay:** passes the signal from its back to its front a moment later.
- **TNT:** a second and a half after it's powered, it blows a hole about five blocks across and throws you if you're close. It sets off any TNT it reaches, and never touches bedrock, the meadow floor, or anywhere you aren't allowed to build.
- **Note block:** plays a note when powered. The block under it picks the pitch.

A door next to power swings open, and shuts again when the power goes. Circuits tick ten times a second. NOT gates and delays make clocks, and wire is an OR. In a room only lever flips and button presses are sent; everyone's screen works out the rest from them, so everyone sees the same circuit. **Pistons and observers.** Three more redstone parts are in the picker:

- **Piston:** when powered, it pushes the blocks in front of it one block along, up to 12 at a time, and pulls back when the power goes.
- **Sticky piston:** the same, but its green face drags the block in front back with it.
- **Observer:** watches the block in front of its face. The moment that block changes, it gives a short pulse out of the red light on its back.

Pistons push the way you face when you place them. Place one while looking straight down and it pushes up; place one looking straight up and it pushes down. Observers watch the way you face in the same way.

What a piston can and can't move:

- Wire, levers, buttons, plates, gates and carpets in the way get crushed.
- Bedrock, obsidian, beds, doors, signs, zip line posts, ostrich eggs and extended pistons won't move, and nor will anything in a fixed building or the meadow floor.
- A piston pushing into you shoves you along, and one pointing up launches you.
- Breaking a piston's head breaks the piston too.

In single player whatever pistons move is saved. In a room every screen works it out the same way from the levers and buttons, so nothing extra is sent.

The start card has a **Play together** section wherever the game runs. Served by `server.js`, rooms run on the server. Anywhere else (GitHub Pages, any static host, even opened from disk) rooms run peer to peer: the host's browser runs the room and everyone else connects straight to it. ## Rooms

- **Host a room** gives a 5-character code and an invite link (`/?room=CODE`).
- **Join** with the code. The room starts as a copy of the host's island; joiners get the full edit list on arrival.
- **Host** builds anywhere. **Builders** (everyone else) build only on the meadow (`fd <= 24`, any height). The host can **Make admin** / **Remove admin** per player from the pause card; admins build anywhere.
- Edits show up live for everyone. Other players appear as rounded toy figures with name labels and walking animation (position sent 10 times a second, only when it changes).
- Builds made in a room save to the host's island. Joiners' own islands are untouched and come back when they leave.
- When the host leaves, the room closes.

### How it works

The server is the authority. Clients send `{t:"edit",x,y,z,b}`; the server checks the sender's role and the meadow rule, stores the edit, and broadcasts `{t:"edit",...,by}` to everyone including the sender. A rejected edit gets `{t:"revert"}` back so the sender undoes its optimistic change. Role changes are only accepted from the host. Edits are rate limited to 25/s per player (burst 60), rooms hold up to 16 players.

Client side, every player edit still goes through `setBlock()`. In a room it sends instead of saving; confirmed edits come in through `applyRemote()`, which never sends, so there is no loop.

Messages: `host` and `join` (both carry `name` and `skin`), `skin`, `seed` (host uploads its island edits), `leave`, `edit`, `role`, `pos` from clients; `hosted`, `joined`, `snap` (edit snapshot in parts of 3000), `edit`, `revert`, `peer`, `left`, `role`, `skin`, `pos`, `closed`, `error` from the server.

Rooms live in memory. Restarting the server closes them (hosts keep their builds locally).

The room rules live in `public/hub.js`, which both `server.js` and the browser load, so a room follows the same rules wherever it runs.

### Rooms with no server

At start-up the page asks for `/healthz`. If the game server answers, rooms use it. If not, rooms go peer to peer with [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com/) (loaded only when you host or join):

- **Hosting** registers the room code with the free public PeerJS server (`voxelisle-CODE`) and runs `hub.js` in the host's browser. The host talks to it directly; guests connect over a WebRTC data channel.
- **Joining** connects to `voxelisle-CODE`. A wrong code says there's no such room.
- The PeerJS server only introduces players. Game messages go straight between browsers (through PeerJS's relay when a network blocks direct links).
- The room lasts while the host has the game open. When the host leaves or closes the tab, the room closes.

`?p2p=1` forces peer to peer even when a server is there; `?server=wss://…/ws` forces a server. `?peerhost=…&peerport=…&peerpath=…` points at your own PeerJS server.

## Deploy

### GitHub Pages (free, no server)

The workflow in `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes `public/` on every push to `main`. Turn it on once: in the repo go to **Settings → Pages**, and under **Build and deployment → Source** pick **GitHub Actions**. Then run the workflow (or push), and the game is at `https://OWNER.github.io/REPO/`. Rooms there run peer to peer.

Your own domain: in **Settings → Pages → Custom domain** enter it (say `play.example.com`), and at your domain registrar add a `CNAME` record from `play` to `OWNER.github.io`. For a bare domain (`example.com`) add `A` records to `185.199.108.153`, `185.199.109.153`, `185.199.110.153` and `185.199.111.153`. Tick **Enforce HTTPS** once it's offered.

### A game server

Any host with WebSocket support works. The client connects to `/ws` on the same origin, using `wss://` on HTTPS. A `Dockerfile` is included and the server reads `PORT`.

- **Fly.io:** `fly launch` (detects the Dockerfile), then `fly deploy`. Keep it to one machine, since rooms are in memory.
- **Render / Railway:** new web service from the repo, start command `npm start`.

Health check: `GET /healthz`.

To point a copy of the page somewhere else at a server, add `?server=wss://your-host/ws`.
